# Stackd: brand and facts for marketing

Read this before writing any post. Anything not listed under "What the app really does"
must not be claimed.

## The product in one line

Stackd is a poker bankroll tracker and preflop trainer in one iPhone app: log your
sessions, see where you win and lose, and train the spots that cost you money.

Loop the app is built around: play → track → analyze → find leaks → train → improve.

## Launch status (Nov 2026)

- iPhone only. In TestFlight beta; **not yet on the App Store**. Until the App Store link
  exists, calls to action point to **stackdpoker.app** ("link in bio"), never "download now".
- No Android version. No web app.
- Prices are not final: don't mention prices. Plans are named Stackd Free, Stackd Bankroll
  and Stackd Pro.

## What the app really does (only claim these)

Tracking
- Log sessions in seconds, or start a live timer when you sit down (rebuys, breaks,
  switching games without stopping).
- Bankroll graph, profit, hourly rate, win rate, profit per 100 hands (online).
- Results by game, stakes, format (live / online), location, weekday and month.
- Deposits/withdrawals, tips and expenses kept separate from results.
- Import from other trackers or a spreadsheet (CSV), export to CSV.

Training
- Preflop trainer with range charts for 6-max and 8-max, 100 and 200 big blinds
  (opening and facing an open, cash games). After each answer, the chart shows how
  often each action is taken.
- "Why?" on every answer: the reason behind the play in plain words.
- Exploit drills: what changes against tight, loose and sticky players.
- Stackd Rating: earned one spot at a time, tiers from White Chip up.
- Streak Challenge, daily quests, levels, XP and achievements.
- Equity calculator: Hold'em, PLO and PLO5.

Hands
- Record a hand with taps, street by street; the pot and result are worked out.
- Replay it on a table step by step; share it as text or a screen-recorded replay.
- Share a session as a clean picture for your story (buy-in stays private).

Friends (friends only, no public feed)
- Add friends by username or invite link; nothing is shared until you accept.
- Close friends vs friends: you choose who sees sessions, results, amounts, location,
  training streaks and your month on the leaderboard (all friends / close friends / no one).
- Sessions and training streaks post by themselves; hands are posted by choice and
  friends can replay and comment on them. Everything else gets emoji reactions (any emoji).
- Monthly leaderboards: profit, hourly, hours, streak and XP.
- Your bankroll total is never shared.

## Things to never say

- Guaranteed winnings, "beat the game", "become a winning player in X days", profit
  promises, or anything implying Stackd is gambling or offers real-money play
  (it is a tracking and study tool only).
- "GTO solver" or "solves" anything: the charts are preflop range charts, there is no
  postflop solver in the app.
- Postflop training, tournaments/ICM charts, Android, a web version, AI coaching,
  HUD or online-site integration: none exist.
- Fake testimonials, invented user numbers, ratings or reviews.
- Names, logos or footage of poker sites, TV broadcasts, pros or other apps.
- Anything aimed at minors: the audience is adult poker players. Keep responsible play sensible
  (bankroll discipline, taking breaks) and never glamorize chasing losses.

  Don't recolor, stretch or add effects to it.
- Phone frames: rounded (#232030 bezel), soft shadow, as in `screenshots/framed/`.
- Square posts 1080×1080, portrait posts and carousels 1080×1350, stories/reels 1080×1920.

## Voice

- Short, direct, a bit dry; talks like a reg at the table, not a hype account.
- Plain words over jargon; when jargon is used (3-bet, BB, hourly), it is used correctly.
- One idea per post. Hooks are concrete ("Your hourly is lying to you") not clickbait.
- Emoji sparingly (♠ 🔥 💰 at most one or two). No ALL CAPS shouting.
